# Third-party notices

## Ghostty Blackhole

The transparent WebGL black-hole renderer in `ui/overlay-shader.frag` is adapted
from the physical model and shader structure of
[`s0xDk/ghostty-blackhole`](https://github.com/s0xDk/ghostty-blackhole),
particularly its Schwarzschild photon acceleration, leapfrog geodesic
integration, thin accretion-disk crossings, temperature profile and
relativistic beaming model. The morphing disk looks are adapted from the
project's Demo Tour presets, including a Pure Lens look adapted to use the
live desktop capture as its lensed background.
